Cat Paw Games — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Cat Paw Games app ("the app") for Android handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by Y2 Soft ("we", "us"). If a translation differs from this English version, the English version prevails.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or precise location.
  • Your cat profiles, photos, statistics and settings are stored on your device. We cannot see them.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase to measure usage and to fix crashes. These services collect some device and usage data, described below.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Cat names, breeds and figures, play statistics, cracker balance, unlocked games, purchase status and settings (language, theme, sound and game options) are saved locally. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If Android device backup is turned on, your phone may include this data in your own Google account backup; we have no access to that backup.

3. Camera and photos

You can add a photo of your cat. The app opens the camera or the photo picker only when you choose to, and uses only the picture you take or pick. A copy is kept in the app's private storage so it can be shown in the app. Your photos are never uploaded to us. You can refuse or withdraw camera permission at any time in your phone settings; the rest of the app keeps working. The app does not use the microphone.

4. Data collected by third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, language), IP address (from which an approximate location such as country or city may be derived) and your interactions with ads. Rewarded videos are optional: you choose to watch them in exchange for crackers or to open a game for a limited time.

Google Firebase Analytics. Records how the app is used, for example which game was started and whether a rewarded video was watched, together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language and country (derived from IP address). We use this only in aggregate to improve the app.

Google Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent containing technical information (device model, operating system, app version, the state of the app and the error). It contains no photos.

Google Firebase Remote Config. Lets us adjust game values (for example how many crackers a video gives) without an app update. It uses an app-instance identifier.

Google Play. If you buy crackers or ad removal, the payment is processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details; the app only receives confirmation of the purchase.

Expo (app updates). The app may check Expo's update service for small updates. This request includes technical information such as the app version, platform and IP address.

These providers act under their own terms: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Privacy and Security in Firebase · Expo Privacy Policy.

5. Why we use this data (legal bases)

  • To run the app and its features, and to process purchases (performance of a contract).
  • To show ads that fund the free app, and to measure and improve the app and fix crashes (your consent where required by law, otherwise our legitimate interest in providing and improving a free app).

6. Your consent and choices

  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent dialog (Google's consent tool) when the app starts. Personalised ads and analytics are used only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Support → Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Google or Privacy settings under Ads.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only with the services listed above, which process it to provide their services, and when required by law.

8. Retention

  • Data on your device: until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • AdMob, Google Play and Expo: according to their policies linked above.

9. International transfers and security

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

10. Children

The app is intended for adult cat owners (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold accounts or contact details, the simplest way to delete data on your device is to uninstall the app. For analytics data held by Google, contact us and include, if possible, the approximate date you used the app. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
